How it works

Three steps between you and the scroll.

1

You set the rules

Pick the apps eating your time and decide how many opens they get per day. Your limits, enforced by your helpers.

2

Your helpers step in

Hit your limit and a message appears. Warm, sassy, zen, or quietly devastating - you pick the tone. It's a nudge that actually lands.

3

Your streak builds

Every day under your limit adds to your streak. The longer you go, the more you can unlock as perks - seasonal drops, hidden codes, limited backgrounds, and more.
